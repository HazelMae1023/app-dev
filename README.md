# Me Before You 

## About the Movie
**Me Before You** is a poignant romantic drama film released in **2016**, based on the bestselling novel by Jojo Moyes. It tells the story of an unlikely bond formed between two people from completely different worlds.

### Main Characters
* **Louisa "Lou" Clark** – A quirky, cheerful, and fashion-loving young woman hired to be Will's caregiver.
* **Will Traynor** – A formerly adventurous, successful banker who became paralyzed after an accident.

### Key Themes
1. **Love and Transformation:** How two people can completely change each other's perspectives on life.
2. **Autonomy and Choice:** Exploring deep emotional questions about personal freedom and quality of life.
3. **Embrace New Adventures:** Living life outside your comfort zone (remember the *bumblebee tights*! 🐝).
